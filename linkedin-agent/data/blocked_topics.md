# Blocked Topics

Agents must NOT write about or engage with these. Edit freely.

- Partisan politics, elections, religion
- Anything defamatory about named competitors or individuals
- Unverified financial/medical/legal advice
- Personal data of customers or identifiable individuals
- Hype claims we can't substantiate ("our AI replaces your whole marketing team")
- Get-rich-quick / growth-hacking schemes
- NSFW or discriminatory content
- Internal confidential metrics, roadmap, or client names unless explicitly approved
- Any topic flagged by Research as low-confidence (< min_confidence) — these are
  surfaced for review, never auto-written as fact
